# TechNews

TechNews turns feed articles into story cards and offers checked paths for readers to try the reported product.

## Language

**Article**:
One item from a source feed, with its source URL and publication details.

**Story**:
A group of articles judged to cover the same announcement, represented by one card.

**Card snapshot**:
The reader-facing story details saved with a bookmark so the bookmark remains readable after story retention expires.

**Verified link**:
An exact Try it URL that passed the trust policy and returned a successful response when checked. Verification records the check time and does not guarantee future availability.

**Feed snapshot**:
A ranked, immutable set of story IDs used to serve a consistent page of the feed.
